Yes. The design approach is mobile-first and adapts to phone, tablet and desktop screens.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Share your idea or problem first. We can clarify features and scope before development starts.

Yes. Maintenance, bug fixes, redesigns, feature additions and integrations are listed among the services.
